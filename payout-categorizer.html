♪ How to Use This Tool

What This Tool Does

This tool helps the Concord Orchestra treasurer categorize bank deposits from Stripe into QuickBooks categories. When Stripe processes ticket sales and donations through ThunderTix, it periodically sends a payout to our bank account. This tool breaks down each payout into the accounting categories you need for QuickBooks entry.

Understanding the Process

1. ThunderTix: When someone buys tickets or makes a donation, ThunderTix records the order with details about ticket quantity, surcharges, and donations.

2. Stripe: Stripe processes the payment and takes a small fee. It doesn't immediately send money to our bank—instead, it accumulates transactions over several days.

3. Payout: Every week or so, Stripe bundles all recent transactions and sends one lump sum to our bank. This is what shows up in QuickBooks and needs to be categorized.

How to Export the Data

From Stripe:

  1. Log into the Stripe dashboard
  2. Go to Balance → Balance History
  3. Export the CSV (this is your balance_history.csv)

From ThunderTix:

  1. Log into ThunderTix
  2. Go to Reports → Orders Export
  3. IMPORTANT: Check the "Email" checkbox before exporting
  4. Export the CSV (this is your orders_export.csv)

Using the Tool

  1. Drag both CSV files onto the upload area (or click to browse)
  2. Select a payout from the dropdown (most recent at the top)
  3. Review the breakdown showing:
    • Ticket Sales: Base ticket prices (Order Amount - Donation - Fees)
    • Surcharges: Our $1/ticket service fee
    • Stripe Fees: What we pay to Stripe (negative amount)
    • Donations: Additional contributions
  4. Check that the validation passes (calculated total = payout amount)
  5. In QuickBooks, find the corresponding payout transaction in your bank account
  6. Split the transaction into these categories using the amounts shown:
    • Ticket Sales
    • Online Ticket Surcharge
    • Online Transaction Fees:Stripe (negative amount)
    • Contributions:Individual
  7. Click "Copy Memo" and paste into the QuickBooks transaction memo field for record-keeping

Memo Format: The copied memo includes the number of tickets and donations, plus a list of all donors with their donation amounts. Example:
45 tickets; 0 subscriptions; 3 donations - John Smith ($100), Jane Doe ($50), Bob Jones ($25)

Matching Logic

The tool matches Stripe charges to ThunderTix orders using:

  1. Email address (most reliable)
  2. Exact amount (to the cent)
  3. Date (ThunderTix date must be same day or 1 day before Stripe)

Charges with email addresses are matched first to ensure the most reliable matches. Charges without emails are matched afterward using only amount and date.

Understanding the Dates

There are two important dates in Stripe:

  • Created: When the customer paid (matches ThunderTix date)
  • Available On: When funds became available for payout

The tool uses "Available On" to group charges into payouts, but uses "Created" to match with ThunderTix orders. This is why a charge from Dec 5 might not appear until the Dec 12 payout.

Data Notes Section

If you see a "Data Notes" section, it shows orders that couldn't be cleanly divided into Adult ($25 + surcharge), Group ($20 + surcharge), and Youth (free) tickets. This is normal and can happen due to:

  • Exchanges (moving to different seats, no charge)
  • Group discounts or special pricing
  • Data entry variations at point of sale

The amounts are still correctly categorized—this section is just informational so you're aware of these special cases.

Troubleshooting

Unmatched Charges: If some Stripe charges don't match ThunderTix orders, it could be:

  • Email addresses differ between systems
  • Same-day transactions with identical amounts (ambiguous)
  • Missing ThunderTix data

The tool shows potential matches to help you investigate. You may need to manually verify these in QuickBooks.

Validation Failed: If the calculated total doesn't match the payout:

  • Check for unmatched charges (shown in red box)
  • Verify both CSV files are for the correct time period
  • Ensure ThunderTix export includes email addresses
